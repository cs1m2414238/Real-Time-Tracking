## 2. Key Operational Findings & Root Cause Analysis

### Finding 1: Kanpur Depot Maintenance Backlog (Critical Bottleneck)
* **Observation**: Kanpur hosts the second-largest fleet formation (**212 assets**), yet exhibits the lowest operational readiness (**74.2%**) and the highest average vehicle downtime (**14.6 days** vs. 4.2 days fleet average).
* **Root Cause**: Diagnostic query analysis indicates that **18 vehicles have overdue scheduled maintenance** (averaging 28 days past service milestone). Overburdened bay capacity at the depot is creating an intake bottleneck.
* **Impact**: Substantially reduces immediate deployment capacity for Central Command heavy transport units.

### Finding 2: Generator Fleet Maintenance Cost Inflation (+23% Cost Spike)
* **Observation**: Field Diesel Generators account for 22% of total fleet inventory (275 units) but consumed **36.4% of total quarterly maintenance expenditure**.
* **Root Cause**: Generator maintenance cost increased by **+23% over the previous quarter**, whereas recorded operating hours increased by only **+8%**. Analysis of work order parts logs identifies premature failure of alternator assemblies and injector nozzles due to dusty field environments and substandard fuel filtration.

### Finding 3: Bhopal Spare-Parts Deficit Directly Correlating with Asset Degradation
* **Observation**: Bhopal reports **14 critical assets with operational readiness scores below 60%**; **9 of these 14 assets (64.3%)** simultaneously flag a `Low` or `Critical Shortage` spare parts status.
* **Root Cause**: Supply chain delay in procuring specialized hydraulic seals and brake boosters for Armored Personnel Carriers has forced 6 combat vehicles into prolonged static holding.

### Finding 4: Upcoming 14-Day Service Surge (Preventive Maintenance Wave)
* **Observation**: Preventive maintenance schedules require **68 assets (5.4% of total fleet)** to enter scheduled depot service within the next 14 days, including **57 currently operational frontline units**.
* **Risk**: If scheduled simultaneously without phased staggering, frontline operational readiness across tactical transport battalions will temporarily dip to **78.4%**.

1. Elevate overall fleet readiness from **83.0% to 86.8%** within 30 days (exceeding strategic benchmark).
